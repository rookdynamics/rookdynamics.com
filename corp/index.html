Agentic AI

Agents that do the work

A chatbot answers questions. An agent finishes tasks. That difference is where the business value sits. An agent picks up a piece of work, moves it through your data and systems, coordinates with other agents where the process calls for it, and hands back a finished result with a record of what it did.

Rook Dynamics designs and builds those systems for enterprise, security, and law enforcement environments. We run an agent stack of our own across engineering and delivery, so we know where agents fail, what they need before they can be trusted with real work, and how to keep them inside the lines you set.

Control and Visibility

Agents are proliferating across SaaS applications, cloud platforms, and internal development teams. Left alone, that becomes a wave of complexity: many agents, no common context, no consistent policy, no way to account for what any of them did or why.

We build the orchestration and governance layer that keeps a growing agent fleet coherent and auditable. One policy surface, shared context, full trace of every action an agent takes.

Control means an agent can only take the actions it was granted, against the systems it was approved for, and a person can pause or revoke it at any point. Visibility means every action, input, and decision is logged and attributable, so when an auditor, a regulator, or your own leadership asks what happened and who authorized it, the answer is already on file.

Security and Privacy

Adversarial attacks on AI systems are not theoretical. Nation-states and commercial adversaries are targeting government and enterprise deployments right now. You want the AI to do its job. Our job is to keep an adversary from hijacking it while it does.

  • Differential privacy to protect training data and model outputs.
  • Adversarial training to harden models against evasion and poisoning.
  • Red teaming against the models, the agents, and the pipelines around them.
  • Continuous monitoring for drift, abuse, and attack signatures in production.
