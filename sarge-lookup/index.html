Built for officers often working outside cell coverage, Sarge Lookup keeps the full criminal code on the device — no signal needed. Search the way you'd talk to a partner: “domestic battery” pulls up the actual charging statute first, and statute numbers work just as well. Criminal offenses and vehicle and procedure stay in separate sections so the charging statute doesn't get lost.

What's in the app:
  • Zero network use, ever. No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry. Nothing leaves the phone.
  • Suggested terms appear while you type, before you finish the word.
  • Recent searches sit on-device, one tap to run them again.
  • Partial statute numbers work. Type what you remember and the results narrow from there.
  • Each result shows the citation, the exact subsection that matched, and the line of text that got you there.
  • Full statute text reads with its subsection structure intact, a jump-to-subsection menu, and text you can select and copy.
  • VoiceOver and Dynamic Type are supported throughout.

Every results list carries a status badge showing offline mode, which corpus build is loaded, its date, and whether that build has been checked for currency. A stale build gets flagged on screen, not buried in a settings menu. You know exactly how old the law you're reading is before you rely on it.

Sarge Lookup is a reference tool. It is not legal advice, and it does not replace your department's policy or your agency's counsel.
