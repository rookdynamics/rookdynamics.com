Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026

Sarge Lookup collects no data. None.

The app performs no networking of any kind. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no telemetry, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you type, search, read, or do in the app ever leaves your device.

Your recent searches are stored on your device only, so you can re-run them. They are never transmitted anywhere and are deleted when you delete the app.

Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or lose.

If Apple provides us crash reports or install statistics, those come through Apple's own opt-in mechanisms under Apple's privacy policy; the app itself sends nothing.
